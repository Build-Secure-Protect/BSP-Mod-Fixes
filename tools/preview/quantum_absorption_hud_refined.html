Quantum suit readout: the refined pick

Concept B as an always-on plate. It slides in from the left edge when a quantum piece is put on, stays while any piece is worn, and slides off when the last piece comes off. A tab on its right edge collapses it: the plate slides off screen and only the tab stays, on the left edge, with the arrow pointing the other way. Collapsed stays collapsed, through taking the armor off and on and across sessions, until the player opens it again. The hit figures are the headless test's: a cactus for 10, 2.0 after the armor, 0.6 absorbed, 600 AE paid from 950,000,000.

1 Worn, open, at rest

At rest: the charge row is bright, the last hit's row sits dimmed until the next hit. The tab's arrow points left: slide the plate away.

  • When it appears The plate slides in from the left edge the moment any quantum piece is worn and slides off when the last piece comes off. No popping on hits.
  • Flush to the edge It sits against the screen's left edge above the hotbar's left end, so it can slide off cleanly; the chestplate glyph marks whose readout it is.
  • Charge The AE row is the four pieces' charge together, as a gold bar with the percentage, and the full figure on the line beneath. Updated every few ticks from the worn pieces, no packet needed.

2 Taking a hit

On a hit: the DMG row brightens with the new figures, the slice paid flashes as a notch at the charge bar's edge, "-600 AE" floats up and fades. After three seconds the row dims back to the resting state; the figures stay until the next hit.

  • Damage row The hit after the armor is the dull red track, the absorbed share fills in teal, "0.6/2.0" at the right. Only the row changes; the plate itself never moves on a hit.
  • Energy The paid slice is a bright notch no narrower than two pixels (600 AE of 950 million is invisible to scale), the figure beneath counts down, and "-600 AE" floats off the plate.
  • What it costs One packet from the server per absorption (the hit, the absorbed amount, the AE paid). Nothing is sent when nothing was absorbed.

3 Collapsed to the tab

Collapsed: only the 9 by 20 tab stays on the left edge, its arrow pointing right, with a one-pixel gold sliver that still shows the charge level. The tab shows only while a quantum piece is worn.

  • Collapsing and opening While playing, Minecraft gives the mouse to the camera, so a HUD tab cannot be clicked. Two ways in: a key binding in Controls ("Toggle quantum suit readout", unbound by default so it clashes with nothing in the pack) and a mouse click on the tab while any screen is open (the inventory, a chest), where the tab is drawn on top of the screen too.
  • It stays collapsed The state is saved on the client in this mod's client config file, so it survives taking the armor off and on, leaving the server and restarting the game, until the player opens it again.
  • Not worn Nothing is drawn, open or collapsed. Putting a piece on brings back whichever state was saved.
Build: one Forge GUI overlay on the client, gated like F-17 so a dedicated server never loads the class; the charge read from the worn pieces every few ticks; one packet from the server per absorption; a client config value for the collapsed state; a key binding; the tab drawn on screens through Forge's screen render event with its click through the screen mouse event. F-25's action-bar line goes. Smallest window (320 x 240 scaled): the plate is 131 x 40 and clears the hotbar's left end by 18 pixels. Text in the game is Minecraft's own font; this page uses a pixel face of the same feel.
