Quantum armor absorption: three HUD concepts

The hit in every mock-up is the one the headless test dealt: a cactus for 10 on a charged quantum set. The armor leaves 2.0, the suit takes 0.6 of that and pays 600 AE from a charge of 950,000,000. Every concept has to show two things: how much of the hit the suit took, and what that cost against the energy it has. The AE slice is one part in a million and a half, so each concept draws the cost as a notch no narrower than two pixels, with the number beside it.

A Twin bars over the hotbar

The same overlay at four times the size.

  • Where Two bars the width of the hotbar, between the crosshair and the armor row, where vanilla leaves a gap. Nothing else moves.
  • Damage bar The hit after the armor is the whole track in dull red; the share the suit took fills in from the left in teal. Both numbers sit on the bar's ends.
  • Energy bar The suit's charge, all four pieces together, fills the track in gold; the slice paid is a brighter notch at the fill's edge, never under two pixels, with the number at the right.
  • When Appears on a hit, holds for three seconds, fades. Another hit refreshes it. Nothing is drawn when nothing was absorbed.
  • Cost One packet from the server per absorption (hit, absorbed, paid, charge, capacity) and one HUD overlay on the client; the smallest of the three to draw.

B Suit plate by the armor icons

The plate at four times the size.

  • Where A small dark-board plate above the hotbar's left end, beside the armor icons it belongs with; a teal rule on top, the chestplate glyph at the left. Slides in from the edge on a hit.
  • Damage row "DMG": the hit after the armor in dull red, the absorbed share in teal, the figure "0.6/2.0" at the right.
  • Energy row "AE": the suit's charge in gold with the paid slice as a bright notch, "-600" at the right, and the full charge with its percentage on a line beneath, so the player reads the charge too.
  • When Three seconds, then it slides away. The plate is the only one of the three with a frame, so it reads as a card and can later take more rows (a reach or jump card's state, say).
  • Cost The same packet as A; the overlay draws the frame and three rows; the font is Minecraft's own.

C Corner readout, always on while wearing the set

The card at four times the size, with the hit row and the floating figure held.

  • Where A card in the top-right corner, shown the whole time a quantum piece is worn: the suit's charge as a gold bar with its percentage. The player gets the readout the armor never had.
  • On a hit A second row appears under the card: the hit in dull red, the absorbed share in teal, "-0.6" at the right and the words beneath; the paid slice flashes as a notch at the charge bar's edge and "-600 AE" floats up from the card and fades.
  • When The charge card stays; the hit row fades after three seconds. A hit with nothing absorbed shows nothing.
  • Cost The charge needs the client to read the worn pieces every few ticks (it has them; no packet) plus the same hit packet as A; the overlay is the largest of the three. The card's corner would need to agree with any other HUD mod the pack puts there (Jade, map mods).
All three take the same server packet on each absorption (the hit after the armor, the absorbed amount, the AE paid, the suit's charge and capacity) and draw with a Forge GUI overlay on the client, gated like F-17 so a dedicated server never loads the class. The action-bar line of the current F-25 would go. Colours are the BSP dark-board set: panel #10151C, slots #0C0E12, dim #2A2F3A, muted #9AA3B5, teal #19D3B0, gold #FFD23A; in the game the text is Minecraft's font at GUI scale, drawn here with a pixel face of the same feel.
